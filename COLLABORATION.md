# Collaboration Pathways

AiGhost is open to exploratory, non-binding discussions in four areas:

## Product and pilot partners

Evaluate the Ghost application and selected opt-in home-node workflows with a defined pilot scope, user consent, measurable success criteria, support arrangements, and a clear exit process.

## Technical partners

Collaborate on local inference, safe device onboarding, cross-platform node clients, security reviews, and maintainable local-first infrastructure.

## Optical and hardware research partners

Review the Neuroplasma software model and determine whether a small, isolated physical laboratory experiment is technically and economically justified. No physical fibre performance is claimed by the present software demonstration.

## Licensing or strategic technology discussions

A licensing or acquisition discussion would require a verified chain of title, contributor agreements, third-party licence audit, technical due diligence, and a separately negotiated contract. No rights are granted by this public overview.

## What this is not

This document is not a share, security, token, loan, or other financial-product offer. It does not set a valuation, investment amount, investor return, or financial terms. It does not make an offer to buy or sell any asset.
