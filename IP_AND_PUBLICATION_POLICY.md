# Intellectual Property and Publication Policy

## Public showcase boundary

The investor showcase should contain product explanations, high-level architecture, validated evidence summaries, diagrams created specifically for publication, and a clear roadmap. It should not contain the private application source, private libraries, internal deployment files, secrets, API keys, node tokens, private keys, personal data, or non-public infrastructure details.

## Private source repositories

The existing Ghost and ghost-crypto-bank repositories were observed as private during the 2026-10-31 review. Their visibility should remain private unless a separate, deliberate publication decision is made after a full source and history audit.

Do not invite an investor as a collaborator to the existing personal-account private repository merely to let them browse. The repository contains much more than the investor needs, and private-repository access is not a substitute for a scoped diligence room.

## Licence and rights

This showcase contains no software source and grants no licence to copy, modify, distribute, or create derivative works from AiGhost software. Do not add an open-source licence unless the owner deliberately chooses one after reviewing ownership and third-party rights.

Before making any intellectual-property representation to a partner or investor, confirm:

- who created each material component;
- whether any contributors or contractors have rights that require assignment or permission;
- whether third-party libraries, fonts, models, datasets, media, and other assets have compatible licences;
- whether any secrets or confidential data entered public Git history;
- whether any filings or registrations are actually complete (never imply patent or trademark protection without evidence).

## Confidential diligence

A confidentiality agreement can support a controlled review, but it does not make an already-public secret private again. Share only the minimum material necessary, with named recipients, time limits, access logs where available, and a clear return/deletion process.

## Public communication safeguards

Keep technical partnership messaging separate from any future financial offer. Do not publish expected returns, token prices, share percentages, investment amounts, or calls to subscribe before the legal form and applicable disclosure requirements are reviewed. Publicly accessible websites, social posts, videos, and presentations can all be relevant to whether a securities offer is public under Estonian guidance.
